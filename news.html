  • WSJ Music Innovator award ceremony

    Billie Eilish used her moment onstage at the WSJ Magazine 2025 Innovator Awards to call on wealthy people to give back. The “ Birds of a Feather ” singer accepted the Music Innovator Award at Museum of Modern Art in New York City, where she spoke openly: “ We’re in a time right now where the world is really, really bad and really dark, and people need empathy and help more than, kind of, ever, especially in our country… If you have money, it would be great to use it for good things, maybe give it to some people that need it.” She added, “Love you all, but there’s a few people in here that have a lot more money than me. If you’re a billionaire, why are you a billionaire? No hate, but yeah, give your money away, shorties.” Host Stephen Colbert also revealed she would donate $11.5 million from her Hit Me Hard and Soft Tour to support efforts in food equity, climate justice, and fighting the climate crisis.
